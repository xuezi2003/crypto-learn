### 推荐资源 (密码学入门必备)

PS: 部分资料有中文资源，以🇨🇳标注在末尾

#### 公开课

1. Dan Boneh: [Cryptography I in Coursera](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1yx411T78i?share_source=copy_web) 🇨🇳
2. Jonathan Katz: [Cryptography](https://www.coursera.org/learn/cryptography)
3. Shafi Goldwasser: [Cryptography](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//www.youtube.com/playlist%3Flist%3DPL6ogFv-ieghe8MOIcpD6UDtdK-UMHG8oH)

   1. 相较于1-2，3多出了关于Adavanced cryptography的内容(Zero knowledge, GC, FHE, etc.).
4. Mihir Bellare: [Intro. to cryptography](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCvEQ5AE-2-ceQpFLQaUaELg/videos)

   1. Include both low-level primitives (block cipher, PRG, hash function, ...) and high-level primitives (PKE, AE, Commitment, ...).
5. **Bar-Ilan University Winner School on Cryptography**

   > 每年围绕一个领域，邀请该领域最知名的大佬们开课，实属密码学研究必备。可根据自身研究方向看对应年份的视频（各年份主题见附录1）。

#### 教材

1. Jonathan Katz & Yehuda Lindell: Introduction to Modern Cryptography. 3rd Version. 🇨🇳(1st)
2. Dan Boneh & Victor Shoup: A Graduate Course in Applied Cryptography
3. 杨波: 密码学中的可证明安全性

#### 综述

1. [Feng05,软件学报] 可证明安全性理论与方法研究
2. [ZWL+19,密码学报] 公钥密码方案构造及安全证明的知识要点和方法论

#### 可证明安全技术

1. Fuchun Guo: Introduction to Security Reduction 🇨🇳

   1. [安全归约（第一讲）bilibili](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1AK4y177XM/?share_source=copy_web&vd_source=3079a67f069b16477bfeaa69b2055b6b) and other videos from Fuchun
2. Victor Shoup: Sequences of Games: A Tool for Taming Complexity in Security Proofs

   1. Mihir Bellare & Philip Rogaway: The Game-Playing Technique
3. Yehuda Lindell: How to Simulate It: A Tutorial on the Simulation Proof Technique

   1. Tutorials on the Foundations of Cryptography (该书第6章)
