## 会议全称

### 三大密码会

- **CRYPTO:** The International Cryptology Conference
- **EUROCRYPT:** The Annual International Conference on the Theory and Applications of Cryptographic Techniques
- **ASIACRYPT:** The International Conference on the Theory and Application of Cryptology and Information Security

### 四大安全会 (Big4)

- **S&P:** The IEEE Symposium on Security and Privacy
  - 为了避免与同名期刊 IEEE SP Magazine 重复，又称 Oakland。因其每年在 Oakland, USA 举行。
- **CCS:** The ACM Conference on Computer and Communications Security
- **USENIX Security:** USENIX Security Symposium
- **NDSS:** Network and Distributed System Security Symposium

### 密码类会议

- **TCC:** The Theory of Cryptography Conference
- **SCN:** The Conference on Security and Cryptography for Networks
  - 双数年召开，需与同缩写期刊 SCN 区分。
- **PKC:** The International Conference on Public-Key Cryptography
- **CT-RSA:** Cryptophers' Track at the RSA Conference
- **FC:** International Conference on Financial Cryptography and Data Security
  - 区块链方向文章质量高，接近四大安全会议。
- **ACNS:** International Conference on Applied Cryptography and Network Security

### 交流类会议（一般仅接收 Presentation）

- **RWC:** Real World Crypto Symposium
  - 主要为已被其它会议接收的论文。同时，Meta, Apple 等公司会展示其在安全与隐私方面的最新成果。
- **PEPR:** Privacy Engineering Practice and Respect
- **Engima (Paused in 2023):**
  - About security and privacy ideas that matter.
- **CFAIL:** The Conference for Failed Approaches and Insightful Losses in Cryptology
  - 中稿后的文章总是光鲜亮丽，而此会议则专注展示背光面.
- **CSLAW:** Symposium on Computer Science and Law

### 安全类会议

- **Euro S&P:** The IEEE European Symposium on Security and Privacy
- **PETS:** The annual Privacy Enhancing Technologies Symposium
- **ESORICS:** European Symposium on Research in Computer Security
- **ACSAC:** Annual Computer Security Applications Conference
- **AsiaCCS:** The ACM ASIA Conference on Computer and Communications Security
- **AFT:** Advances in Financial Technologies
- **WPES:** Workshop on Privacy in the Electronic Society

### 理论计算机 (TCS) 会议

> 早期密码学顶级文章与计算复杂性密切相关，因此多数发表在 TCS 会议上。

- **FOCS**: IEEE Symposium on Foundations of Computer Science
- **STOC**: Annual ACM Symposium on Theory of Computing
- **SODA**: ACM-SIAM Symposium on Discrete Algorithms
- **ICALP**: International Colloquium on Automata, Languages and Programming
- **ITCS**: The Innovations in Theoretical Computer Science Conference

### 人机交互 (HCI) 会议

> 除技术以外，我们还需要关注密码方案在现实世界的可用性 (usability)。这类文章除了发表在四大外，还会出现在 HCI 会议中。

- **SOUPS:** Usenix Symposium on Usable Privacy and Security
- **CHI:** Conference on Human Factors in Computing Systems
- **UIST:** The ACM Symposium on User Interface Software and Technology
- **CSCW:** The ACM Conference on Computer Supported Cooperative Work

## (公钥) 密码学顶级期刊

> 这里仅列举了有关公钥密码方向的期刊。在对称密码方案设计与硬件实现上，亦有部分期刊质量较高。

- **JoC:** Journal of Cryptology
  - 大部分文章为各个密码会已接收文章的扩充版本。
- **CiC:** IACR Communication in Cryptology
  - 2024 创刊，从编委来看，前景较好。
- **DCC:** Designs, Codes and Cryptography
